## 2024-05-17 - Accessible Names for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons and links are completely inaccessible to screen readers unless they have an `aria-label` or accessible text within them. Sighted users can also benefit from a `title` attribute which provides a native tooltip on hover.
**Action:** Always provide an `aria-label` for screen readers and a `title` attribute for tooltips on buttons, anchor links, or other interactive elements that rely solely on iconography to convey meaning.
